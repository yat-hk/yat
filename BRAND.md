# YAT Brand Brief

## Brand Overview

**Brand name:** YAT  
**Brand mark:** YAT 日  
**Category:** AI-powered ambient e‑ink display platform  
**Primary expansion:** Your Ambient Terminal  
**Hidden meaning:** Yet Another Terminal  
**Cantonese connection:** 日 represents “day,” reflecting YAT’s role in everyday life.

YAT is a calm, intelligent display platform designed around life in Hong Kong. It brings together the information people need—weather, transport, schedules, reminders, news and personalized AI summaries—on a quiet, low-power e‑ink screen.

YAT is Hong Kong-first, but not Hong Kong-only.

## Brand Idea

**Technology that informs without interrupting.**

Most screens compete for attention. YAT does the opposite. It quietly organizes what matters and keeps it visible without notifications, glowing screens or endless scrolling.

YAT turns scattered information into one calm, useful view.

## Purpose

To make everyday information calmer, clearer and more human.

## Vision

A future where useful technology lives naturally in the background, helping people without constantly demanding their attention.

## Mission

Build an open, intelligent display platform that transforms local services, personal information and digital tools into simple, glanceable experiences.

## Positioning

For people who want to stay informed without spending more time on their phones, YAT is an AI-powered ambient e‑ink platform that presents the right information at the right moment.

Unlike conventional dashboards and smart displays, YAT is designed to be quiet, persistent, energy-efficient and deeply connected to local life.

## Brand Promise

**The information you need, without another glowing screen.**

## Target Audiences

### Everyday users

Hong Kong residents who want a calmer way to check weather, transport, appointments, reminders, headlines and household information.

### Professionals

People who want focused, glanceable information on their desk without introducing another distracting screen.

### Families

Households that need a shared view of calendars, school schedules, weather, reminders and important updates.

### Developers and makers

People who want to build custom widgets, integrations and e‑ink experiences using an open platform.

### Local businesses

Cafés, studios, shops, clinics and offices that need attractive, automatically updated information displays.

## Product Principles

### Calm by default

YAT should reduce digital noise rather than create more of it.

### Useful at a glance

People should understand the most important information within a few seconds.

### Intelligent, not intrusive

AI should summarize, prioritize, translate and organize quietly in the background. It should not dominate the experience.

### Local by design

YAT should understand Hong Kong’s languages, services, routines and density of everyday life.

### Personal and adaptable

Every display should reflect the needs of its owner, location and moment.

### Open and extensible

Developers and users should be able to create their own layouts, widgets and integrations.

### Trustworthy

Important information should be accurate, clearly sourced and under the user’s control.

## Brand Personality

YAT is:

- Calm, but not passive
- Intelligent, but not showy
- Minimal, but not cold
- Local, but not exclusionary
- Playful, but not childish
- Technical, but still approachable

If YAT were a person, it would be the thoughtful Hong Kong friend who already checked the weather, knows when the next bus is coming and gives you the short version without wasting your time.

## Brand Voice

### Clear

Use short, direct language. Make complicated information easy to understand.

### Quietly confident

Do not exaggerate or rely on futuristic claims. Let usefulness speak for itself.

### Human

Sound natural and considerate, not robotic or overly corporate.

### Locally aware

Use English and Traditional Chinese naturally. Cantonese expressions can add warmth when appropriate, but should never feel forced.

### Occasionally witty

The “Yet Another Terminal” meaning reflects a subtle sense of humour. This should appear as an Easter egg for developers rather than the main consumer message.

## Messaging Hierarchy

### Primary message

YAT gives you the information you need in one calm, always-visible view.

### Supporting messages

- Personalized by AI
- Easy on the eyes and light on energy
- Useful without being distracting
- Customizable through widgets and integrations
- Open to developers and makers

## Short Description

YAT is an AI-powered e‑ink display platform that turns weather, transport, calendars, reminders and local information into one calm, personalized view.

## Elevator Pitch

YAT is a customizable e‑ink display built for everyday life in Hong Kong. It connects to the information and services you already use, then uses AI to organize what matters into a quiet, always-visible dashboard. No glowing screen, no endless scrolling—just the right information at a glance.

## The Meaning of YAT

YAT has several layers:

- **YAT 日** represents the day and YAT’s place in everyday life.
- **Your Ambient Terminal** describes the customer experience: a personal information surface that lives quietly in the background.
- **Yet Another Terminal** is the developer Easter egg—a playful acknowledgement that YAT is also an open, programmable technology platform.

The brand should lead with **YAT** rather than repeatedly explaining the acronym. The layered meanings should be discovered gradually.

## Visual Direction

The visual identity should feel like modern print rather than conventional consumer electronics.

### Suggested characteristics

- Strong black-and-white foundation
- Warm paper-white backgrounds
- One restrained accent colour for status and identity
- Editorial typography with excellent Traditional Chinese support
- Generous spacing and clear information hierarchy
- Simple geometric icons that reproduce well on e‑ink
- Layouts inspired by newspapers, transit signage and printed schedules
- Subtle references to Hong Kong rather than obvious skyline imagery

### Logo direction

The primary wordmark should be simple: **YAT**.

The character **日** can act as a secondary symbol, app icon or frame around the wordmark. Its rectangular form naturally resembles both an e‑ink display and a window into the day.

Possible lockups:

- YAT
- YAT 日
- YAT — Your Ambient Terminal

### Avoid

- Neon AI gradients
- Robot imagery
- Excessive “sparkle” symbols
- Generic smart-home aesthetics
- Overly futuristic typography
- Tourist clichés such as dense skyline or junk-boat imagery
- Visual elements that cannot reproduce clearly on monochrome e‑ink

## AI Philosophy

YAT should not feel like a chatbot attached to a display.

AI works behind the interface to:

- Select and prioritize useful information
- Summarize long content
- Translate between English and Traditional Chinese
- Adapt layouts to routines and preferences
- Combine information from multiple services
- Create personalized widgets and briefings
- Explain why important information is being shown

The principle is simple:

**AI does the work; YAT shows the result.**

## Brand Architecture

> **Aspirational namespace reservation only.** None of these exist, none are
> committed, and several (YAT Cloud, YAT Store) would contradict the current
> architecture's no-backend, no-accounts stance if built naively — any future
> service would have to earn its way past that principle, not around it. This
> list exists so the names stay coherent if the project grows.

Potential product and service names:

- **YAT One** — flagship home or desk display
- **YAT Mini** — compact personal display
- **YAT Frame** — larger wall-mounted display
- **YAT Studio** — layout and widget builder
- **YAT Cloud** — synchronization and AI services
- **YAT Store** — widget and integration marketplace
- **YAT OS** — software platform for compatible devices
- **YAT for Business** — commercial display offering
- **YAT Developer** — APIs, SDKs and documentation

## Competitive Difference

YAT should not win by having the longest feature list. It should win by being more relevant, more local and more thoughtful.

Its differentiation comes from the combination of:

1. Hong Kong-focused integrations and bilingual experiences
2. AI that reduces information rather than generating more noise
3. A calm e‑ink interface
4. An open platform for custom widgets and hardware
5. A distinctive local brand with international potential

## Brand Essence

**One calm view of what matters.**
